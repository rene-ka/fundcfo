# Roadmap

## 0.5: structure (current)

Instructions and data separated: one data root (`~/Documents/FundCFO` or `FUNDCFO_HOME`) with projects, handbook, tasks, process templates, CFO skills list, assistant and auto modes, versioned data with upgrades.

## 0.6: content

- Knowledge from fundcfo.co mapped into CFO skills, process templates and references.
- First process templates from that material: quarterly reporting, with portfolio-company updates and the management letter as sub-processes.
- Credit fundcfo.co as the content source.

## Later

- **Onboarding**: one question at a time, surveying the files first, with visible progress. Ends with a read-back: "tell me about my setup".
- **Discover and flag**: notice mismatches between what the handbook says and what happens in daily work.
- **CFO 360**: periodic high-level review of tasks, report cycle, risks, regulation, fund lifecycle.
- **Nudges on the Menu**: suggestions such as checking the quarterly report, exploring the setup, reviewing periodic tasks.
- **Task wrap-up**: close tasks that are essentially done and move the remainder to the backlog; park or escalate tasks that linger.
- **Permission matrix**: what FundCFO may do on its own, per mode and type of action.
- **Reference materials and standards**: ILPA, AICPA, CFA Institute.
- **Email and Slack.**
- **Connect tasks and log to other systems**: keep the task list or the log in a tool the team already uses, such as a task tracker, instead of only in Markdown.
- **Data root as a git repository**: history and backup for free, and a sandboxed agent can clone it at the start and push at the end.
- **Plugin marketplace**: install and update from this repo in Claude Code.
- **Full auto-update**: install new versions without manual steps.

## Known gaps

What FundCFO says it covers versus what it has instructions for:

- **Judgment**: little guidance yet on how a CFO should handle situations outside the live skills.
- **Technical process templates**: none yet for areas such as US tax reporting, PFIC or UBTI.
